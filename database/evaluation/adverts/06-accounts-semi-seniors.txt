About the position
Job Overview:

We are seeking to recruit two ambitious, qualified, and detail-oriented Accounts Semi-Senior to join our Compilation Team. The ideal candidates will have a strong background in accounting, excellent technical skills, hands-on experience working within a reputable accounting firm, and a strong commitment to integrity and professional ethics.

Key Responsibilities:

Prepares management accounts and compiles financial statements for clients.
Reviews and analyzes financial data to identify discrepancies or errors, ensuring accuracy and compliance with accounting standards and regulations.
Handles day-to-day bookkeeping, accounting functions, and maintain accurate records and files using standard accounting packages and MS Excel spreadsheets.
Ensures key information is provided by the client in order to complete the job effectively.
Monitors time records for specific assignments, highlight actual or likely overruns, and take effective ownership of assignments.
Effectively utilizes IT systems, develops skills where necessary, and suggests improvements to management.
Prepare and present financial reports while developing and maintaining strong professional relationships with clients.
Qualifications and Requirements:

Education and Professional Status: ZICA Licentiate Member.
Experience: Minimum of 3 years of relevant work experience.
Firm Background: Prior working experience with a reputable accounting firm is required.
Technical Skills: High proficiency in MS Excel spreadsheets and familiarity with various accounting packages.
Personal and Professional Attributes: Good analytical skills, high integrity and ethics, ability to meet strict deadlines, capability to adapt to change quickly and multi-task, and good interpersonal and relationship-building skills.
