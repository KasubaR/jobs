Description:

Job Summary
The PCR Technologist will support the provision of high-quality molecular laboratory testing services for the Anti-Retroviral Treatment (ART) programme. The position will support Viral Load (VL), Early Infant Diagnosis (EID), Human Papilloma Virus (HPV), and HIV Drug Resistance Testing (DRT).
Main duties
Perform molecular testing for VL, EID, HPV and HIV Drug Resistance Testing in accordance with approved procedures and standards.
Ensure accurate and timely reporting of laboratory results through DISA and other nationally approved Laboratory Information Systems.
Implement and maintain laboratory Quality Management Systems (QMS) in line with ISO 15189 principles, including internal quality control and external quality assurance requirements.
Monitor and manage laboratory reagents and consumables using e-LMIS to ensure uninterrupted testing services.
Provide technical support and mentorship to supported facilities on specimen collection, packaging, storage, transportation and results management.
Participate in external quality assessment programmes and ensure timely testing and submission of results to relevant assessing agencies.
Strengthen collaboration and referral networks with PCR laboratories and other relevant laboratory services.
Qualifications
A Diploma in Biomedical Sciences with at least three (3) years’ relevant work experience. A Bachelor’s degree in Biomedical Sciences or a related field will be an added advantage.
A valid practicing license with the Health Professions Council of Zambia (HPCZ).
At least two (2) years’ relevant laboratory experience in a PCR/molecular laboratory.
Experience implementing laboratory Quality Management Systems, with knowledge of ISO 15189.
Training in SLMTA/SLIPTA will be an added advantage.
Training in Biosafety and Biosecurity and/or National Rapid HIV Testing will be an added advantage.
Good computer skills and ability to work with relevant laboratory information systems.
Ability to work effectively with minimum supervision.
Strong technical knowledge of molecular/PCR laboratory procedures.
Strong commitment to laboratory quality, accuracy and biosafety.
Good analytical and problem-solving skills.
Effective communication and mentorship skills.
Strong organizational skills and attention to detail.
Suitably qualified candidates are invited to apply. However, only shortlisted candidates will be contacted.
