JOB OPPORTUNITIES – NAVA AVOCADO LIMITED
CAREER OPPORTUNITIES
Nava Avocado Limited invites suitably qualified and experienced candidates to apply for the following positions:

1. DIVISION IN-CHARGE – AVOCADO PRODUCTION
Minimum 15 years’ commercial fruit-production experience, including avocado, with at least 3 years managing avocado production at senior level.

Hands-on expertise in Hass/Fuerte cultivars, orchard management, irrigation, nutrition, crop protection, harvesting, budgeting and GlobalG.A.P. required.

Must hold a recognised Remote Pilot Licence/Certificate and have agricultural drone experience in spraying, mapping and multispectral monitoring.

Strong team leadership and knowledge-transfer skills essential.

2. HEAD – MARKETING
Relevant tertiary qualification and minimum 15 years’ experience in avocado/fresh-produce marketing, exports and commercial operations.

Proven expertise in large-scale avocado export programmes, European retail markets, multi-farm supply coordination, packhouse operations, cold-chain logistics, pricing, negotiation and profitability management.

Experience developing and commissioning packhouse/cold-chain capacity essential.

3. FINANCIAL CONTROLLER
Qualified Chartered Accountant with minimum 10 years’ progressive finance, accounting, audit and commercial experience, including large-scale agriculture/agribusiness.

Strong expertise in IFRS reporting, MIS, budgeting, forecasting, cash flow, internal controls, audits, taxation, statutory compliance and commercial risk.

Experience with international lender/development-finance reporting and SAP or equivalent ERP systems required.
