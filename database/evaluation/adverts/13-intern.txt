ENERGY REGULATION BOARD (ERB)
INTERNSHIP ANNOUNCEMENT
Job Title: Intern x 2 (Lusaka)

Reports to: Senior Manager Director General’s Office – Lusaka

Background
The Energy Regulation Board (ERB) is a statutory body established under the Energy Regulation Act, No. 12 of 2019, to regulate the energy sector by licensing undertakings and ensuring consumers receive quality products and services while ensuring a reasonable rate of return to the energy utilities.

The ERB now seeks to recruit two (2) dynamic and suitably qualified people to fill the positions of Monitoring & Evaluation (M&E) Intern at its head office in Lusaka.

Job Purpose
To provide operational support to the Monitoring & Evaluation unit.

Main Duties and Responsibilities
Supporting the Monitoring and Evaluation of departmental work plans.

Supporting the coordination of the Strategic Business Plan (SBP) development activities.

Review and analysis of documents and performance information.

Supporting data collection, collation and validation.

Drafting of correspondences and reports.

Any other M&E-related assignments as may be assigned by the unit.

Minimum Qualifications
Full Grade 12 School Certificate or equivalent.

A minimum of a degree in Demography, Development Studies, Economics, Public Administration or any related fields.

Preferably a resident of Lusaka.

Applicants must have graduated not earlier than 1st January 2023.

Duration of Internship: Initial six (6) months with the possibility of an extension.
